02

Copywriting

1 — Attention-Grabbing Hook

Open a loop in the very first line. One bold, curious headline that makes the scroll stop and the reader need the answer.

2 — Content Development

Write to one reader, not a crowd. Name who it’s for, then earn trust with proof: real names, real numbers, real places.

3 — Clear Value Proposition

Lead with what they gain, never what we offer. Every benefit is concrete, short and easy to scan, so saying yes feels obvious.

4 — Soft Call to Action

Invite, don’t push. One clear next step with a friendly nudge, so the reader acts because they want to, not because they’re told to.
